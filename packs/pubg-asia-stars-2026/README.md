# PUBG Asia Stars 2026 — Live Case

A live, evidence-first Causari case reconstructing the PUBG Asia Stars 2026 controversy involving Himass and TanVuu.

## Why this is a Causari case

This pack combines capabilities already proven elsewhere in Causari:

- **World Cup 2026:** event-driven live updates;
- **Vietnam E-commerce Compliance 2026:** disposable audience/views over canonical data;
- **OpenAI–Hugging Face Agent Incident 2026:** explicit separation of events, claims, evidence and sources.

The central rule is:

> **An official finding is a sourced claim by an authority, not an independent Causari adjudication.**

## Layers

1. `events.json` — what happened in the public timeline;
2. `claims.json` — atomic assertions with epistemic status;
3. `evidence.json` — support/limits tied to sources;
4. `sources.json` — provenance records and source tier;
5. `links.json` — causal relations between events only;
6. `insights.json` — reusable patterns learned from the case;
7. `views.json` — disposable Canvas projections (`overview`, `official-record`, `evidence-and-gaps`, `what-changed`, `review-and-appeal`).

## Source policy

Primary PUBG/KRAFTON notices are authoritative for **what PUBG/KRAFTON officially stated or decided**. They are not automatically treated as independent proof of every underlying factual proposition.

Media sources are used for chronology and attributed team responses. `justiceforpubgvn.com` is treated as an advocacy source; it is useful for questions and public-evidence gaps but is never promoted to canonical truth merely because it is detailed.

As of **24 September 2026**, the sources indexed here do not establish a final outcome for the players' explanation/appeal process. That status must be rechecked when the case is updated.
