# Geopolitical Risk Timeline — 2026 pilot

Research-preview pack for the Iran–US crisis. This is **not** a nuclear-war prediction or an emergency alert.

## Epistemic boundaries
- `events.json`: dated, attributable reporting events, not unverified downstream conclusions.
- `claims.json`: atomic propositions and their epistemic status.
- `evidence.json` and `sources.json`: provenance and verification limitations.
- `views.json`: projection and hypothetical branches, not a causal graph.
- `links.json`: empty until defensible event-to-event causality is established.
- `forecasts.json`: empty: no calibrated, resolvable prediction has been entered.

Source articles are starting references, not a substitute for reviewing their primary materials. Secondary reporting is explicitly labeled. The exact time and operational details of reported military strikes require additional corroboration. This pack does not reproduce or endorse the user-shared fallout graphic.

## Promotion policy
All pack events are `pack-only` by default. Global promotion requires a separate source review and canonical deduplication. No changes to global Canvas or Career Radar.
