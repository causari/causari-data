Observed

Directly supported by a primary source or independent investigation.

Inferred

A causal or explanatory interpretation supported by evidence, but not directly observed as a controlled fact.

Speculative

A useful interpretation or hypothesis that should not be promoted to fact.

Causari's job is not to make every edge look certain. It is to show where certainty ends.
